# Contributor licensing plan / 贡献者授权方案

Maintune ships source under AGPL-3.0-only and may separately license rights controlled by mcxianyujun under commercial terms. A normal inbound AGPL contribution does not by itself provide the rights needed to relicense that contribution under proprietary terms.

Preview uses a minimal, manual process:

1. Every contributor confirms authorship, third-party provenance, and authority to contribute under AGPL-3.0-only.
2. Ordinary fixes may be accepted into the AGPL distribution only.
3. A contribution needed in the dual-licensed core is held until a project-specific CLA is signed separately.
4. The CLA keeps the contributor's copyright while granting the project owner enough non-exclusive rights to sublicense and relicense the contribution under AGPL and alternative commercial terms.
5. Employer authorization is requested when employment or contract terms may control the contribution.
6. Signed agreements, legal names, addresses, and signatures remain in private records. The public repository stores only a non-sensitive acceptance record if needed.
7. Preview does not install or depend on a third-party CLA SaaS. Automation may be considered only after separate privacy and operational approval.

The draft agreement is [CONTRIBUTOR-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md](../../CONTRIBUTOR-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md). It requires legal review before production use. This plan follows the general pattern used by the Apache Software Foundation: contributors keep their rights while granting sufficient distribution rights, and employer interests may require a corporate agreement. See the official [ASF contributor agreements](https://www.apache.org/licenses/contributor-agreements.html) and [CLA FAQ](https://www.apache.org/licenses/cla-faq.html).

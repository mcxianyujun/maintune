# Artwork attribution

No third-party artwork is included.

## Candidates reviewed

| Candidate | Source | Evidence found | Missing evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Official Vsinger character images | `vsinger.com` | Rights holder and fan-creation guidance can be identified | No explicit permission found to redistribute the original image files inside an open-source repository | Excluded |
| Official Luo Tianyi Japanese website images | `luotianyi.jp` | Official public display | Public display does not grant repository redistribution | Excluded |
| Community illustrations and model renders | Various creator platforms | Some allow personal or non-commercial derivative use | Original-file redistribution and sublicensing terms were absent or inconsistent | Excluded |

Review date: 2026-09-17.

The empty asset slot is intentional. Search-engine copies, video screenshots, promotional posters, album art and artwork with only general fan-creation permission are not acceptable sources.
